# Motion — Video Editing & Creative Post-Production

> **"YOU HAVE 3 SECONDS. I MAKE THEM COUNT."**  
> *Reels, travel films, YouTube edits, and brand content cut to hold attention.*

**Motion** is a high-performance creative post-production website representing freelance video editor Mayank Bisht. Designed for content creators, YouTubers, influencers, and brands seeking retention-driven video editing.

## 2. Core Service Offerings

1. **Short-Form Content**
   - Fast, punchy edits for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, ads, and creator clips.
   - Hook-first structure, Captions & sound design, Platform-ready exports.
2. **YouTube & Long Form**
   - Clean narrative edits for talking-head videos, vlogs, travel films, interviews, and explainers.
   - Story pacing, B-roll selection, Music, mix & polish.
3. **Brand & Promo Edits**
   - Launch videos, product promos, event recaps, and agency client edits that feel premium.
   - Color grading, Motion graphics, Revision-friendly workflow.
